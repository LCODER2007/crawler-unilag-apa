# Privacy Notice — URAAS

**Last updated:** 2026-06-13
**Data controller:** University of Lagos Library (in partnership with the Africa PID Alliance)
**Contact:** dataprotection@unilag.edu.ng · library@unilag.edu.ng

This notice explains how the University Repository Analytics & Assessment System (URAAS) processes
personal data, in line with the **Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA)** and the supervisory
guidance of the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

## 1. What URAAS does

URAAS aggregates and analyses **already-published scholarly metadata** — research papers, theses,
datasets and cultural-heritage items — produced by African universities, to provide research
intelligence (publication trends, collaboration patterns, framework alignment, and special-collections
analytics). It does **not** create new profiles of individuals beyond what is already public in the
scholarly record.

## 2. Personal data we process

| Data | Source | Purpose |
|------|--------|---------|
| Author / researcher names | OpenAlex (CC0), Crossref, ORCID, institutional repository (OAI-PMH) | Attribution, collaboration analysis, author disambiguation |
| Institutional affiliation | Same as above | Institutional analytics, intra-African collaboration |
| ORCID identifier | ORCID public API | Author disambiguation |
| Publication metadata (title, year, DOI, abstract, citations) | Public bibliographic sources | Analytics |

We process **only data already made public** by the authors through scholarly publication or their
public ORCID record. We do **not** collect special-category (sensitive) personal data, and we do not
scrape private or restricted sources.

## 3. Lawful basis (NDPA 2023)

Processing is carried out for **educational, research and public-interest purposes** — analysis of a
university's own and peer institutions' published scholarly output. This falls within the NDPA's
recognised grounds for processing for research and public-interest archiving, applied with the
safeguards below. "Publicly available" is not treated as a blanket exemption; we still minimise data
and honour data-subject rights.

## 4. Safeguards

- **Data minimisation** — only the bibliographic fields needed for analytics are stored.
- **No sensitive data** — special-category data is never collected.
- **Source compliance** — harvesting respects each source's terms and `robots.txt`; we use the
  OAI-PMH and public APIs intended for metadata reuse.
- **Access control** — the dashboard is authenticated; bulk exports and the staff directory are
  restricted to authorised administrators; full-text files are served only where rights permit.
- **Retention** — bibliographic records are retained for as long as they remain part of the scholarly
  record; correction and removal requests are honoured (see §5).

## 5. Your rights

If you are a researcher whose metadata appears in URAAS, you may request **access, correction, or
removal** of your personal data, or object to its processing. Contact **dataprotection@unilag.edu.ng**.
We aim to respond within the timeframe required by the NDPA. You also have the right to lodge a
complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

## 6. Persistent identifiers

URAAS may mint persistent identifiers (DocID, ARK) for items. These reference scholarly works, not
individuals, and are used for citation and resolution only.

## 7. Changes

This notice may be updated as the system evolves; the "Last updated" date above reflects the current
version.
